EXPEDITION - how to play
========================

Start: double-click Expedition.exe in this folder (keep the folder together).

"Windows protected your PC"? The game is not code-signed, so Windows warns the
first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Controls
  W A S D        move
  Mouse          aim; click or hold to shoot (Space also shoots)
  R              reload
  Esc            pause (resume, settings, quit to menu)
  F1             help        F11  fullscreen

Goal: reach the glowing beacon in each sector. Shoot alien nests before they
flood the room. Armories hold ammo and health; aliens sometimes drop ammo.
Out of ammo? Your sidearm never runs dry - it is just slow.

Your settings and records are saved in %APPDATA%\Expedition.
To remove the game, delete this folder and that one.

If the game ever crashes, please send the file %APPDATA%\Expedition\crash.log.
